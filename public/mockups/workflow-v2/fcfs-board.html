How FCFS Rank Works

First-come, first-served: Jobs are ranked by the order they were created. The job created first gets rank #1 and has priority. If two installers are double-booked, #1 keeps its slot — #3 is the one to be re-assigned.

Clash detection: Two jobs clash when they share the same formally-assigned installer AND their times overlap on the same day. Sales "suggestions" (yellow cards) do not count as clashes — only formal green assignments do.

Re-assign button: Takes the scheduler/coordinator directly to the job form of the lower-priority job to pick a different installer.
