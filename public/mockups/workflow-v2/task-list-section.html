Task List — Job Form Section

Optional section · editable by sales and coordinator · consumed by sub-installers on site
Optional: If no tasks are added, this section collapses and shows an "Add task list" button. Tasks are drag-reorderable. Sub-installers see the same list as interactive checkboxes with a progress bar — they tick off each step as they go.
