Multi-store jobs — the simple version, round 4

A project is a container with labels. It copies nothing. Jobs keep their own forms; the container just groups them.

  • Round 4: project folders get a green ring + a side punctuality stripe (split when mixed) — list and month
  • Round 4: month project pill opens a floating pop-out (no giant cells); finished jobs go grey, not struck out
  • Round 4: completed jobs stay in the day under the veil; one Schedule page with an All / Scheduled / Pending / Completed filter — the Completed (and Pending) tabs retire
  • Round 4: project punctuality is only a default for new jobs, unset by default — every job keeps its own

Everything grey, green-ringed or dashed is tappable — try the demo buttons and the month pills

Screen 4 · Office roles, PC / tablet

Month view — Notion style, tap a project pill for its pop-out

Project pills carry the green ring and a folder mark; their stripe splits red/blue when that day's nested jobs mix punctuality. Tap a project pill — a floating card pops out beside it; the calendar cell itself never grows. The pop-out always stays fully on screen: bottom rows open it upward (try ABC on the 23rd or 30th), edge columns shift it inward, and it never slips under the top bar, the bottom nav, or the floating assistant / bug buttons — it layers above them like every overlay in the app. Tap a job row inside → that job's details; "Open in list" → that day unfolded. Finished jobs turn grey with no colour stripe — no strike-through. Tap a single job pill → the job. Tap the day number → that day in the list.
