Coordinator — Clash Prompt Modal

Triggered when the scheduler detects a double-booked installer. Two variants shown.

How this triggers: When sales pushes a job to schedule and an installer is already assigned to another job at the same time, the system shows this modal to the coordinator. The higher FCFS-ranked job is protected. The coordinator must reassign the installer on the lower-ranked job, or push anyway.
