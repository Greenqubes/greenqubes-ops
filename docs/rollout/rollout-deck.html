04Access

Your role decides what you see

Everyone gets an account with one of seven roles. The system shows each person exactly what their job needs — nothing more, nothing missing.

Sales

Creates jobs, pushes them to the schedule, suggests installers, manages clients.

Scheduler

Runs the company-wide schedule, assigns installers, resolves clashes.

Coordinator

Same scheduling powers as the scheduler, working per job alongside sales.

Installer

Sees only their own assigned jobs. Photos, task lists, chat, DO signing.

Designer

Views job details and joins the job chat — read-only on everything else.

Production

Updates production status, DO issued, instructions and production photos.

Admin

Nic — accounts, roles and system health.

05In the office

Sales, Scheduler, Coordinator

Sales

Create it, push it, suggest

New job form with client dropdowns, file buckets (Permit-to-Work, BCA, Designer JO), and an AI Suggest button for writing. A yellow installer suggestion tells the scheduler who you'd pick — but only a scheduler or coordinator can confirm it.

Scheduler

The FCFS board

Jobs ranked in the order they were pushed — first come, first served. Installer bars show punctuality: blue means a flexible window, red means strict on-time. Clashes light up before they become real problems.

Coordinator

Per-job control

Coordinators are attached to specific jobs and can formally assign installers on them, keep the chat moving, and get the same alerts the office does.

Everyone in office

Live everywhere

Schedule, board and job forms update in real time. If someone changes a job while you're editing it, the app warns you instead of losing your typing.

06Studio and site

Designer, Production, Installer

Designer

See everything, break nothing

Full view of the job and its files, plus the job chat. No save buttons to press by accident.

Production

Your fields only

Mark production ready, DO issued, keep production instructions and photos current. Everything else stays read-only.

Installer

My Jobs

Today, up next, this week. Each job: location, time, task list to tick off, chat with voice notes, and the camera button for completion photos. No commercial figures — ever.

Outside helpers

No account needed

External installers get a personal lifetime link — they accept or decline jobs, see details and tick tasks from that page. Sub-installers get a "supporting role" ping.

07Notifications

Telegram does the chasing

Connect once (today), and the right messages find you. Nobody has to remember to tell anybody anything.

  • ▸Job assigned — installers get pinged the moment a job is formally theirs, with date, time and location.
  • ▸New job needs an installer — schedulers get pinged when sales pushes a job.
  • ▸Chat messages — unread job-chat messages arrive as a tidy count, at most one ping a minute.
  • ▸Overdue alerts — twice a day, jobs past their date nudge exactly the people on that job.
  • ▸Monday digest — subscribers get a weekly summary of important company know-how, with a vote on what gets kept.

09Right now

Get set up — three steps

Sign in with Google

Go to greenqubes-ops.vercel.app and sign in with the Google email you gave Nic. Your name and role are already waiting for you.

Connect Telegram

Tap your profile picture (top right) → Connect Telegram. Telegram opens — press START. The bot replies "your Telegram is now connected".

Check your name

Open the profile menu again — you should see your name, and "Telegram connected" with a tick. That's it. You're done.

Stuck? If the app says your account isn't set up, your sign-in email doesn't match the one Nic has — put your hand up and we'll fix it on the spot.
10House rules

Three rules, then questions

  • 1The app is the truth. If a job isn't on the schedule, it isn't scheduled. Check the app, not the group chat.
  • 2No photos, no completion. A job only closes with completion photos attached. Only the scheduler can override that.
  • 3Yellow is a suggestion, green is real. A yellow installer name is sales' suggestion; the job is only yours when it's green — and that's when Telegram tells you.

Questions — now, or anytime: ask Nic.
