Sales

create it, push it, suggest who installs itGreenqubes

What you'll see

  • The full Schedule (list, week, month) and the FCFS board
  • Your own Pending jobs before they're pushed
  • All clients and contacts, with add-new built into the dropdowns

What you do

  • Create jobs — details, client, files in named buckets (Permit-to-Work, BCA, Designer JO), AI Suggest for writing
  • Push to Schedule — the system checks installer double-bookings first
  • Suggest installers (yellow) — scheduler or coordinator confirms
  • Duplicate a similar past job instead of retyping it

Telegram pings you when

  • An installer is formally assigned to your job
  • Your job's chat has unread messages
  • A job you're in charge of goes overdue

Remember

  • Yellow = your suggestion. Green = actually assigned.
  • You're the job's person-in-charge unless someone changes it
  • Installers never see prices — chat accordingly

Set up once — rollout day

  1. Sign in at greenqubes-ops.vercel.app with the Google email you gave Nic
  2. Tap your profile picture (top right) → Connect Telegram → press START
  3. Reopen the menu — it should say Telegram connected
Stuck? "Account not set up" means your sign-in email doesn't match what Nic has — tell Nic. Anything else: use the Report Bug button, bottom of any page.

Scheduler

the company schedule is yoursGreenqubes

What you'll see

  • Everything — every job, every status, every role's activity
  • The FCFS board: jobs ranked by push order, installer bars coloured by punctuality (blue = flexible, red = strict)
  • All overdue alerts company-wide, not just your own jobs

What you do

  • Formally assign installers (green) — from the job form or the board's assignment panel; Save & Notify fires the Telegrams
  • Resolve clashes — the board flags double-bookings; you decide: reassign, shift the time, or save anyway
  • Confirm sales' yellow suggestions and external-installer suggestions
  • Complete jobs — and you're the only one who can complete without photos (use sparingly)

Telegram pings you when

  • Sales pushes a job — "New Job — Assign Installer"
  • A coordinator flags a clash for review
  • Job chats have unread messages; any job goes overdue

Remember

  • First come, first served — the board's order is the fairness rule
  • An installer only learns about a job when you make it green

Coordinator

scheduler powers, on your jobsGreenqubes

What you'll see

  • The full Schedule and FCFS board
  • The jobs you're attached to as coordinator (a job can have several coordinators)

What you do

  • Formally assign installers (green) on your jobs — same power as the scheduler
  • Confirm or clear sales' yellow suggestions
  • Editing onto a clash? Choose Alert Scheduler & Save or re-assign yourself
  • Keep the job chat moving between office and site

Telegram pings you when

  • An installer is assigned on one of your jobs
  • Your jobs' chats have unread messages
  • One of your jobs goes overdue

Remember

  • Assigning fires the Telegrams — installer, sales, and fellow coordinators all get told automatically
  • When in doubt on a clash, alert the scheduler rather than forcing it

Installer

your jobs, your phone, nothing extraGreenqubes

What you'll see

  • My Jobs — Today, Up next, This week, Completed
  • Each job: location, time, the files you need, the task list, the chat
  • Only jobs assigned to you — and never any pricing

What you do

  • Tick the task list as you work — the office sees progress live
  • Chat with the office — type, voice note, or camera
  • Upload completion photos — the job cannot close without them
  • Sign the DO; add late photos to past jobs if you forgot on the day

Telegram pings you when

  • A job is assigned to you — with date, time, location
  • Your job's chat has unread messages
  • An unfinished job of yours goes overdue

Remember

  • No Telegram ping, no job — if it's not in My Jobs, it's not yours yet
  • Photos before you leave site — it's the only way to close the job
  • You can switch the app to 中文 from your profile menu

Designer

see everything, break nothingGreenqubes

Production

your fields, kept currentGreenqubes

Remember

  • Everything outside your production fields is view-only for you — that's by design
  • "Production ready" is what tells scheduling the job can actually happen — keep it honest
